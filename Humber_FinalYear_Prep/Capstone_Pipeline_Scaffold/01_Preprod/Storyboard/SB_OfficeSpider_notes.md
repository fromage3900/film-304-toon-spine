# Office Spider — Storyboard

**Artist:** Jeffrey Dawn
**Date:** 2026-10-05
**Pages:** 3
**Format:** Hand-drawn pencil thumbnail storyboard

## Summary

A comedic slice-of-life short about an office worker whose day is interrupted by a spider. The story follows a classic three-act structure: mundane routine → disruption → confrontation.
